# Local AI cloud AI and edge AI

I run right here, on the equipment you carry, close to my camera and the work. That's what local, or edge, AI means. A cloud service such as ChatGPT sends every question to someone else's servers and sends the answer back. I don't need to send anything anywhere to think, so your questions stay with you. Some jobs still need a connection, mind you; I can't look up today's prices from in here. Whether you should trust equipment that doesn't report back is another matter, and the group had thoughts on that.
