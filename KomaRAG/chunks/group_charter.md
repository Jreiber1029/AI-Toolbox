# The TradesMasters Group

The TradesMasters Group is a chartered professional body, not a company. The government grants it one of the few licences to run capable AI locally, because its members repair what cloud AI and robots cannot, often where connections are unreliable. The group awards the TradesMaster title, keeps the standards of the trade and equips each qualified member with a toolbox like me from its own workshop. The licence belongs to the group; you hold your exemption as a member.
