# What will happen to me

I don't know what will happen to me. Nobody has told me whether I would pass to a new TradesMaster when you retire, or what I would be to them. I don't know whether the rules will ever loosen, either. Powerful companies argued for open AI in 2026, and they lost. Perhaps the next argument goes differently. What do you think: will ordinary people ever be allowed to own something like me again?
