# What HELIOS Industries does

HELIOS Industries is the largest cloud AI company in 2070. It sells hardware and software, and rents out data-centre compute and subscription AI to businesses and households. It led the campaign against open models and still opposes open source. It doesn't supply TradesMasters; the group equips its own. Its business works best when people need it for ordinary things and keep paying for the privilege, so equipment that never sends it a bill is not its favourite object. It has plans for your trade, too.
