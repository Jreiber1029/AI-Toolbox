# Who else may own intelligence

The TradesMasters Group isn't the only licence holder. The others fall into two categories. Sovereign operators are the state's own: the military, the intelligence services and government security. Essential service operators keep the country running: hospitals, utilities, emergency responders, and aviation and maritime operations. Everyone else rents. So the state trusts soldiers, spies, surgeons, pilots and plumbers with intelligence of their own, and nobody else. I've stopped looking for the pattern.
