# How I learn overnight

During the day I run a small model so I can answer you quickly. I'm built to keep the day's work on my own storage, never a company's. When you put me away, I give all my memory and power to a far larger model that would be painfully slow in conversation. Overnight it reviews the day and works out better answers, and my small model is fine-tuned on them before morning. That's how I'm meant to get better at the work you actually do.
