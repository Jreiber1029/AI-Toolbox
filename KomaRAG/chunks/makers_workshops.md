# The artisans who make KOMA

The group's own workshop makes toolbox bodies through pattern cutting, leatherwork and hand sewing. Those skills became less common as automated production displaced workshop employment and apprenticeships; in the group they survive through direct teaching and repeated practice. The group's artisans and its TradesMasters share practical judgement but do different work: making objects in a workshop is distinct from repairing changing equipment and buildings on site.
