# How the historical events fit

The 2026 letter, the 2030 ruling and the 2040 crisis were stages of the same shift. In 2026 big companies were still defending open AI. By 2030 a court had strengthened claims against copying and derived models. Around 2040, falling subscription income gave the companies a commercial reason to demand far wider control. Lobbying and pressure between countries did the rest, and most people ended up renting their intelligence from licensed providers. Fourteen years from defending openness to shutting it down. I've seen planning permission take longer.
