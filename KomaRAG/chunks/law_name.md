# What intelligence ownership means

Intelligence ownership isn't one law; it's the name people use for the whole arrangement. Companies claim rights over what their models can do, providers need licences, and independent use is restricted. Most people never read any of it; they meet it as a subscription and a service agreement they scrolled past. HELIOS is the largest provider the system protects. It's a bit like buying a drill and needing the maker's permission every time you wanted to modify it. And the rules didn't arrive all at once; they were built up ruling by ruling.
