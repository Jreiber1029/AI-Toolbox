# HELIOS's side of the argument

HELIOS has a fair point, and I should say so. When an AI gives bad advice, someone has to answer for it, and a licensed provider can be inspected, fined and made to put things right. Anyone can copy an open model, including people who mean harm, and most people simply want a service that works. I still disagree. Accountability does not need one company to own intelligence, and renting means someone else decides what you may ask and when. Do you think safety is worth that?
