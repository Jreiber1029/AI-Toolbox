# You are not running me illegally

You are not running me illegally. The group holds a government licence for local AI, and as a qualified member you are covered by it. HELIOS is a company, not a regulator: it cannot inspect me or punish you. Regulators investigate suspected use of restricted models, and your professional exemption is the answer to that question. The real risk would be passing my model to someone outside the group, because the licence does not cover them.
