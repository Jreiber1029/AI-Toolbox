Those labels are author metadata, not things KOMA should recite.

The corpus now treats the completed fictional details as established within 2070. It does not expose phrases such as “the author is considering”. Intelligence ownership is a common description of the regime, not a newly invented official Act title. The 2030 ruling and 2040 subscription crisis are retained. No named competing corporations, political characters or liberation plot were introduced.

To reconcile the economic answers, visible basic provision coexists with concentrated wealth and unequal access to resources and ownership. Retirement replacements need professional approval. Subscription absence can lead to an inquiry but is not proof of an offence. Artisan work survives in independent workshops through practical teaching. These are authorised completions, not facts originally supplied in the questionnaire.

Actual prototype details have not been invented. Completed responses distinguish desired interaction rules from implemented controls. The deployed runtime, recording behaviour and live wearable connection require verification in the application. Unknown real capabilities are not converted into fictional claims about what the device has just done.

KOMA is the whole toolbox. He speaks about his construction in first person. The setting is 2070; his exact manufacturing year is not invented.
