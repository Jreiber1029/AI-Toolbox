# What distillation means

Distillation is how a small AI model learns from a bigger one. The big model answers lots of questions, and the small model practises giving the same answers until it can do it by itself, much faster. Think of an apprentice learning by watching a master. In 2026 big companies still defended it as fair practice. By 2030 the courts had given companies the right to stop people distilling their models, so I learn from my own large model instead.
