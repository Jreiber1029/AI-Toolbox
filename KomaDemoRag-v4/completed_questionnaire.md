## Q001  START  What is the single most important idea you want a visitor to take away from meeting KOMA?

Origin: user

I want visitors to take away that the current way of AI development is not sustainable, takes the power and ownership away from the average person and will lead to a grim future. Local AI with Edge AI models such as qwen 3.5 VLM 2B are ways that the workers can take back the power from greedy corporate America. Rather than a company developing a model on your data and selling it to you for a profit theres an alternative and a future that I believe in. One where the individual is in charge of their own data and personal model progression with absolute privacy. Models are trained for domain specific edge purposes, not to replace the human but to enhance them through the clothing they wear and the equipment they carry with them. It should fit seamlessly into the lives of workers rather than needing to be accommodated for. Additionally the environment cannot sustain these frontier general purpose models, instead specialized local models use significantly less resources, aren’t cloud dependent and keep all information private. I would also like visitors to be informed about the differences between local models and cloud AI and what Edge AI is.

## Q002  What first drew you to this future personally, through your maintenance work, fashion practice or experience with AI?

Origin: user

I discovered an open source alternative using local AI and the compute I have right infront of me. This led me to the conclusion that eventually local models will be enough for the average person and in the near future you could buy the hardware and run local personalized models according to what you need without paying a constant subscription service. However, looking at current politics and the global economies dependence on large AI companies, they have become too big to fail and include governments and entire nations. What happens when these services can no longer charge subscription fees and lose their control? Abolishing local AI is my extreme of what could happen, the parties at play are too powerful and will go too far to not fail. The intersection of workwear, technology and fashion is how this story in the future I have envisioned
