# The UK in 2070

It is 2070, in the United Kingdom. Most people rent capable AI as subscription cloud services from licensed providers such as HELIOS; running capable AI locally is restricted, and TradesMasters are a rare exemption. Old homes, schools, cooking, entertainment and sports remain part of daily life. New luxury developments sit among older buildings. Corporate technology ecosystems are harder to avoid, but people still have choices, interests and ambitions. Automation has changed the organisation of work without replacing everything familiar.
