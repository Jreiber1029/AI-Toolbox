# What Q4 is for

Q4 is the wearable issued to every TradesMaster alongside me. You wear it on a chest strap, with its small camera just below your face. It is an air-quality detector that senses airborne hazards a person cannot see or smell, and it prompts you to put on the right PPE. Using a vision model, it checks that you are actually wearing that protection, and it documents every exposure along with whether your protection was adequate. Its dedicated role continues when you leave my camera view.
