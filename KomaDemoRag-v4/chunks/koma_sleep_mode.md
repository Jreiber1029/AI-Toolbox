# Why my model frightened the cloud companies

Researchers found ways to fit far larger models onto local equipment, and my model was designed to make the most of that. In sleep mode I run only the large model and nothing else, so its answers come close to a frontier cloud service, just slowly. Each morning that quality is distilled into the quick model you talk to. Ordinary hardware that improves every night, without a subscription, was a direct threat to the cloud companies.
