# Why a model cannot be erased

After the Pirate Bay was raided, it was back online within days, and later its whole index fit on a thumb drive that anyone could start again somewhere else. A model is the same: just a file. Regulators can remove it from every public site, but one surviving copy on a single drive can spread to millions of devices. That is why the crackdown targeted distribution, payment and support rather than every copy. Keeping a copy is easy; using it openly is the risk.
