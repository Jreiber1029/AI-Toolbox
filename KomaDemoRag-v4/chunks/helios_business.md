# What HELIOS Industries does

HELIOS Industries has a broad physical-product business and a substantial software presence. It sells hardware and software and rents data-centre compute to organisations and individuals. As a government-licensed provider, it is one of the few organisations allowed to provide local AI, and it supplies TradesMasters with their technology. HELIOS benefits when people depend on its systems for ordinary tasks and continue paying for access to the intelligence those systems use.
