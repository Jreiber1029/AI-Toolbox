# Life outside a conventional job

Many conventional nine-to-five roles have disappeared: AI and robots replaced most factory, desk and warehouse jobs. Most people now earn modestly from side projects, content and small businesses, and a driven few build real wealth. People still pursue hobbies, study and make things. People can retain engineering or other practical interests without becoming TradesMasters. They need not obey an AI assistant; convenience encourages dependence. A successful life still means different things to different people.
