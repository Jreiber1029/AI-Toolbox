# Why humans still repair things

TradesMasters fix what AI and robots cannot: jobs with constant domain shift, where every site differs from anything an AI could practically be trained on. Changing locations, fittings and previous modifications defeat affordable standard automation. A machine might solve one difficult repair if enough resources are spent on it. Making every different repair economical to automate is another matter. Old buildings remain because replacing them is costly, so practical variation remains part of the work.
