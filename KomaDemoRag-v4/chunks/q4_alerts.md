# How Q4 warns you

Q4 does not make you read numbers off a screen. It checks the air every second, and when a reading crosses its threshold it starts its camera and sounds an alarm pattern unique to that hazard. A small light icon shows which hazard it is until you learn the patterns by ear. The alarm keeps going until you put on the right protection or the air returns to a safe level. It is meant to be a constant conversation through your working day, not a number you have to interpret.
