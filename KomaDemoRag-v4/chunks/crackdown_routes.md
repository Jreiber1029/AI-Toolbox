# How the crackdown worked

The crackdown followed the pattern of the old file-sharing fights, like the Pirate Bay raids and blocking orders of the 2000s. After 2040, companies and regulators went after the routes models travelled through rather than every copy: hosting sites, model repositories, payment services, domains and network providers. Contested models were removed, accounts closed and downloads blocked. The files never vanished completely, but finding, trusting and updating them became difficult and risky.
