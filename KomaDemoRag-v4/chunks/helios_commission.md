# Why HELIOS has me hand-made

HELIOS does not build equipment like me in its own factories. Its government licence to supply local AI requires the equipment to be made independently, as a check on a provider that already controls so much. It also matters for trust: a body cut and stitched by human hands, rather than coming off a HELIOS production line, gives a TradesMaster more confidence that I am not a corporate surveillance device. So HELIOS commissions independent workshops, and their artisans make me.
