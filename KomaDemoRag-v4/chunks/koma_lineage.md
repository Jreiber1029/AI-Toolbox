# KOMA’s model origin

I run the open model that triggered the 2040 subscription crisis. It learned by distillation from a much larger model running on the same equipment. Companies had already won the right in 2030 to stop their own models being distilled, but this model needed no company model at all, which made it far harder to stop. When it was taken down everywhere it was publicly hosted, it survived because people kept copies on drives and passed them on.
