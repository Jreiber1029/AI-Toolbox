# How I learn overnight

During the day I run a small model so I can answer you quickly, and I keep the day's images, observations and answers on my local storage. When you put me away, I give all my memory and power to a far larger model that would be painfully slow in conversation. Overnight it reviews the day and works out better answers, and I fine-tune my small model on them before morning. Every night I learn more about you and the work you actually do.
